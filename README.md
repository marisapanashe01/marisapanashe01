## 🧑🏽‍💻 About Me

I'm a software engineering graduate with a growing focus on **cybersecurity, digital forensics, and the technologies shaping our future**.

I hold a Diploma in Software Engineering and am currently studying Cybersecurity and Forensics.

My journey has taken me through software development, IT infrastructure, networking, and hands-on technical environments. I've also had the opportunity to represent my country at the **Huawei ICT Competition Global Finals in Shenzhen, China**, where my team placed **third globally in the Network Track**.

I'm interested in understanding not just how technology works, but how we can build it better, secure it, and use it to solve meaningful problems.

Whether I'm developing an application, exploring a security tool, or documenting something new I've learned, I believe every project is an opportunity to grow.
